## What is Kling AI?

Kling AI is a professional-grade AI video generation platform developed by Kuaishou, the Chinese technology company behind the popular Kwai short video platform. This lineage is significant—Kuaishou brings substantial AI research capabilities and infrastructure to bear on video generation, resulting in a platform that competes directly with Western alternatives at competitive price points.

The platform specializes in producing high-quality videos from text prompts with natural motion and realistic physics. What distinguishes Kling from competitors is its professional feature set: consistent character generation, lip sync capabilities, camera control, and the motion brush tool for precise animation control. These features position Kling for serious creative and commercial applications rather than casual experimentation.

The introduction of Kling 3.0 brought significant quality improvements and expanded capabilities. The platform now supports multiple aspect ratios suitable for different platforms and use cases, from social media to commercial production.
