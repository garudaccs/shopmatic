## What is Make (Integromat)?

Make, formerly known as Integromat, has established itself as the visual automation platform that enables businesses to connect applications and automate workflows without coding. With over 3,000 app integrations and a powerful visual workflow builder, Make handles everything from simple automations to complex enterprise workflows with equal elegance.

What distinguishes Make from competitors is its visual approach to automation. Rather than writing code or configuring technical triggers, users build workflows by connecting visual modules that represent app actions and operations. This approach makes automation accessible to non-technical users while remaining powerful enough for developers to build sophisticated solutions.

The platform has expanded significantly from its early days as a simple automation tool. Make now supports AI-powered workflows, real-time monitoring, error handling, and team collaboration features that make it suitable for serious business automation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is Make free to use?**
Yes, Make offers a free tier with 1,000 operations per month and access to 100+ apps. Sufficient for learning and light automation needs.

**Q2: How do operations work?**
Operations are individual actions within a workflow—each module execution counts as one operation. A workflow with three modules connecting two apps consumes three operations per run.

**Q3: Can I use Make for complex enterprise workflows?**
Yes, Make handles sophisticated workflows including conditional logic, data transformation, loops, and error handling. Enterprise plans provide additional scale and support.

**Q4: How does AI integration work?**
Make supports AI modules that can process data, generate content, and make decisions within workflows. This enables intelligent automation that adapts to context.

**Q5: What happens if an automation fails?**
Make provides comprehensive error handling with retry logic, notifications, and fallback paths. You can configure exactly how failures are handled.
