## What is Brevo (Sendinblue)?

Brevo, formerly known as Sendinblue, has evolved from a transactional email service into a comprehensive marketing platform serving businesses of all sizes. The rebrand reflected a broader vision: providing an all-in-one solution for customer engagement through email, SMS, live chat, and marketing automation from a single platform.

What makes Brevo particularly attractive is its generous free tier. Unlike competitors that offer severely limited free plans, Brevo includes 300 emails per day indefinitely along with core features like marketing automation, landing pages, and CRM. This makes it accessible for small businesses and startups just beginning their marketing journey.

The platform strikes a balance between ease of use and sophisticated capabilities. Newcomers can send their first campaign within minutes using the intuitive interface, while advanced users appreciate the automation workflows, segmentation, and A/B testing features needed for optimization.

## Key Features

📧 **Email Marketing Campaigns** - Create, send, and track professional email campaigns with a drag-and-drop editor, customizable templates, and detailed analytics.

📱 **SMS Marketing Automation** - Reach customers directly with SMS campaigns and automated messages, integrated seamlessly with email for multi-channel journeys.

⚙️ **Marketing Automation Workflows** - Build sophisticated automation sequences that trigger based on customer behavior, demographics, and lifecycle stages.

💬 **Live Chat for Websites** - Real-time customer conversations through a customizable chat widget that integrates with your CRM for complete customer context.

📘 **Facebook Ads Integration** - Create and manage Facebook ad campaigns directly within Brevo, syncing contact data for better targeting.

📄 **Landing Page Builder** - Create conversion-focused landing pages without coding, complete with forms and integrations for lead capture.

📬 **Transactional Emails** - Send order confirmations, password resets, and other transactional emails with high deliverability and reliability.

👥 **Contact Management and Segmentation** - Organize contacts with custom properties and create targeted segments for personalized campaigns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is Brevo really free?**

**Q2: How is Brevo's email deliverability?**
Brevo maintains strong deliverability rates through proper authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), sender reputation management, and list hygiene tools. Paid plans include dedicated IPs for additional control.

**Q3: Can I send SMS campaigns with Brevo?**
Yes, SMS marketing is available on all plans with pay-per-send pricing. SMS integrates with email for multi-channel campaigns and automation journeys.

**Q4: Does Brevo support marketing automation?**
Yes, Brevo includes marketing automation on Business plans and above. You can create automation workflows based on behavior, time, and contact properties.

**Q5: What integrations does Brevo offer?**
Brevo integrates with major e-commerce platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento), CRM systems, WordPress, and offers API access for custom integrations.
