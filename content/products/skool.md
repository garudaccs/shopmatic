## What is Skool?

Skool is a community platform purpose-built for creators, coaches, and educators who want to build engaged communities around their expertise. Founded by Dan Nicholson, creator of Leadpages, Skool emerges from a creator's perspective—what would a community platform look like if designed by someone who actually tried to build and monetize a community?

The platform combines course delivery with community features in a surprisingly minimal package. Where competitors offer overwhelming feature sets requiring significant setup and management, Skool focuses on doing a few things exceptionally well. This simplicity is intentional: Skool believes community building shouldn't require technical expertise or extensive management overhead.

What makes Skool particularly interesting is its business-model alignment: the platform is designed to help creators monetize communities profitably. The combination of community features, course delivery, and payment processing creates a complete ecosystem for community-based businesses.
