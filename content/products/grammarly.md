## What is Grammarly?

Grammarly has become the ubiquitous writing assistant, used by millions of professionals, students, and organizations worldwide to improve their written communication. Founded in 2009, the platform has evolved from a simple grammar checker to a comprehensive AI-powered writing assistant that addresses clarity, engagement, delivery, and correctness across virtually any writing context.

What sets Grammarly apart is its seamless integration throughout your writing workflow. Whether you're drafting emails in Gmail, writing documents in Microsoft Word, composing tweets, or working in custom applications, Grammarly works wherever you type. This ubiquity has made it an essential tool for anyone who communicates professionally in writing.

The platform's AI goes beyond basic spell-checking to provide contextual suggestions for grammar, punctuation, style, tone, and clarity. The premium version adds features like plagiarism detection, genre-specific goals, and advanced AI writing assistance that helps transform rough drafts into polished, professional communication.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Is Grammarly actually accurate?**
Grammarly uses advanced AI trained on vast amounts of text, resulting in high accuracy for common errors. However, no tool catches everything, and context-sensitive suggestions may not always fit your intended meaning. Always review suggestions critically.

**Q2: Does Grammarly work offline?**
The browser extension requires internet connectivity for AI processing. Some basic checking may work offline, but full functionality requires an internet connection.

**Q3: Is my writing data safe with Grammarly?**
Grammarly states that they don't sell your writing data and use it only to improve their services. Enterprise and Business plans offer additional privacy controls and compliance features.

**Q4: Can Grammarly replace human editors?**
Grammarly catches many errors and style issues but can't replace human judgment for nuanced content, structural improvements, or domain-specific accuracy. It's an excellent first pass, not a complete replacement for professional editing.

**Q5: How does plagiarism detection work?**
Grammarly compares your text against billions of web pages, academic papers, and other sources. It highlights potential matches and provides links to original sources for review.
